**A two-stage workflow calibrates spatial models against longitudinal SUM-159 observations.** **A,** Recorded lineage and oxygen histories, with chromosome-sampling proxies; the highlighted 2N O1 A18 passage links to E and F2. **B,** Pooled single-cell chromosome counts, colored by the two genomic-state clusters. **C,** Measured CPU use and allocated CPU-hours for each fit, excluding local preparation and reporting. **D,** Living nonWGD fractions under hypoxia, with O1/O2 trajectories, molecular observations and Wilson 95% intervals, fitted proxies, and the dotted lineage reference. **E,** A lineage-model example of measurements and distributions entering the weighted cost: total retained live + dead count (0.5), and dead count, coverage, relative area, gap and Clark–Evans organization (0.1 each). Count comparisons average two seeds; spatial illustrations use one seed and four observed fields. **F,** Four matched acquisition groups, each showing brightfield, segmentation and both models in representative crops covering 25% of the field. Brightfield and segmentation share a registered crop; independent model fields have separately selected crops. Fitted statistics use full fields. Scale bars, 100 pixels. Segmentation colors indicate living (green) and dead (orange) cells; model colors indicate nonWGD (blue), WGD (pink) and dead (orange), referring to founder identity for lineage and current genomic state for perspective, without cell-to-cell correspondence. Perspective native fields are independent realizations; microscopy was evaluated at fitted mixtures, and composition was rechecked separately. These are calibration comparisons, not prospective validation.
